What changes the answer

  • Every learner pays every month. Refunds, free trials, drop-outs and discounts will lower the figures.
  • Negative numbers mean the package loses money at that size once support staff and the reserve are counted.

How this is calculated

  • Group tutors: paid per class hour, plus 10% extra hours for make-up classes. One tutor covers about 12 teaching hours a week. A two-subject plan puts each learner in two classes.
  • Private tutors: a share of every fee, with one tutor covering about 12 taught hours a week.
  • Stripe: 2.9% + CA$0.30 on domestic cards, 5.7% + CA$0.30 on international cards, plus 0.5% for failed payments.
  • Lessonspace: cheapest plan for the month's room hours at US$1 = CA$1.4262. Group rooms with more than 3 learners also pay an extra US$0.25 per person per hour.
  • TutorBird: US$16.95 plus US$4.95 for each tutor and staff member.
  • Marketing: nothing under 50 learners, then CA$100 rising to CA$500 a month. Reserve: CA$150 rising with size.
  • Tax: a flat 11.2% placeholder on what is left. An accountant sets the real figure, including HST and instalments.

Good to know

Questions parents ask

How much does Private 1-On-1 cost?

Private 1-On-1 is priced per learner, per month: CA$139 for one weekly session or CA$269 for two. Prices in your currency are estimates, and billing is in CAD. Extra subjects or siblings are quoted on enquiry.

Which time zones do you support?

ClassOf5 works with families in North America and in the UK and Europe. Tell us where you are based and we will arrange times that suit you.

How will I know how my learner is doing?

Parent visibility is central to ClassOf5. Tell us what you would like to follow, and we will agree how progress is shared with you.

Can my learner join a small group later?

That is something we can talk through. Tell us what you have in mind and we will explain the options, including the small-group plans.

What happens after I send an enquiry?

We will get in touch to discuss your learner's goals, subject and schedule, then agree the best next step together.

How the number is worked out

  1. Children to grade bands. Your shares split each package's children across the six bands.
  2. Enrolments to sections. In each band, divide enrolments by the section maximum and round up. A sixth child in a band of five opens a second section. A pair plan puts each child in two sections.
  3. Sections to hours. Each section meets twice a week for 60 minutes. Add the make-up allowance. Private sessions are 1 hour each.
  4. Bands to pods. Alpha and Beta (Grades 1 to 4) share tutors, Grades 5 to 8 share tutors, and Grades 9 to 12 share tutors.
  5. Hours to tutors. A tutor teaches Mon to Sat, 3 sections a day at most, so 18 hours a week. Every tutor holds at least one section, 2 hours a week. In each pod, divide hours by 18 and round up.
  • Section size moves the answer most. Spreading learners across six bands makes sections smaller than the maximum of 5, and every small section needs a full tutor hour.
  • Subjects are not split. In practice a Maths section and an English section cannot be merged, so real section counts can be a little higher.
  • A Maths tutor cannot cover an English section, so check tutors per subject within each pod before you hire.
  • Tutors cost about CA$7 a month each in TutorBird. The package calculator adds the costs.

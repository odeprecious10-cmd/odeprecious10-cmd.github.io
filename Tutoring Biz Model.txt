Before mapping the exact step-by-step workflow, there is a crucial technical detail to keep in mind regarding ClassIn:

ClassIn's free tier caps classes at **3 students** (4 total participants including the teacher) and **40 minutes per lesson**. Because your model relies on **5 students per class** for **60 minutes**, the free plan will lock out your 4th and 5th students and cut the lesson off mid-way.

**To keep fixed software costs under $40/month while keeping ClassIn:**

* Use **ClassIn's Pay-As-You-Go Credit Top-Up** (a $15–$20 prepaid account balance). This removes the participant/time limits and costs only a few cents per class, letting you use ClassIn without paying a heavy monthly subscription. *(Alternatively, standard Zoom Pro at $15/mo or free Google Meet links work identically inside this map).*

### Step-by-Step Operational Workflow

#### 1. Parent Self-Registration (Teachworks Form)

* You embed a **Teachworks Custom Enrollment Form** on your website.
* The form lists your **6 weekly time slots** (e.g., *Math Level 1 — Tuesdays @ 4:00 PM*).
* **Hard Roster Cap:** Teachworks is set to cap registration at **5 students per section**. Once 5 parents sign up for a slot, the system automatically closes that class time or switches it to a waitlist.

#### 2. Payment & Autopay Lock ($60/month)

* Within the enrollment form, the parent enters their credit card via **Stripe**.
* Stripe automatically creates a recurring **$60/month subscription**.
* If a credit card fails on the 1st of the month, Teachworks auto-sends a billing update link to the parent and flags the profile before the next lesson.

#### 3. Automated ClassIn Delivery

* In Teachworks, you create your 6 recurring weekly calendar events.
* You paste each class's dedicated **ClassIn Room Link** into the "Location / Video Link" field.
* **Automated Email/SMS Reminders:** Teachworks sends an automated reminder 24 hours prior to class:
> *"Hi [Parent Name], [Child Name]'s class is tomorrow at 4:00 PM. Click here to join the ClassIn room: [Link]"*



#### 4. Teaching & Attendance (1 Minute of Admin)

* The tutor logs into ClassIn and runs the interactive 60-minute session for the 5 students.
* Immediately after class, the tutor opens the Teachworks dashboard on their phone or laptop, opens the lesson, and checks off attendance (`Attended` or `Absent`).
* This single action updates student records and automatically calculates tutor payroll hours.

#### 5. Self-Service Makeup Class Engine (Zero Admin Friction)

When a child is sick or misses a class:

1. The parent logs into their **Teachworks Parent Portal** and cancels the upcoming lesson at least 2 hours in advance.
2. Teachworks automatically grants **1 Makeup Credit** to the student's profile.
3. The parent uses their portal to browse your other 5 weekly class times, finds a session with an open slot (fewer than 5 kids), and books the makeup session.

* **Result:** You never have to manually reschedule, swap links, or answer late-night attendance texts.

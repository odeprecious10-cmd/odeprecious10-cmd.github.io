Before you enroll

How Standard and Flex work

When does a Standard section open?

We start with the published time and open more Standard sections as parent demand supports them. A full section closes to new registrations, and the next time is chosen from the strongest demand.

What does Flex mean?

Flex offers more approved recurring time windows. It is not unlimited custom scheduling: families choose from the times shown, which lets us match schedules while keeping classes small and consistent.

Can my child take Math and English together?

Yes. Math and English registration open in parallel for cohorts being launched. Each subject has its own section status, so one can be ready while the other is still forming. Two classes never overlap.

Can Flex families reschedule?

Flex includes one schedule transfer per month into another active section of the same subject and level, subject to an available seat. It does not create a new one-off lesson. If no seat is available, the normal makeup process applies.

What if a class is still forming?

NovaPoint will give you a clear decision date and an alternative, such as another suitable active time or the applicable refund, rather than leaving you waiting.

Are all subjects twice a week?

Math, English, Chemistry and Physics run two 60-minute live classes a week. Robotics & Coding is one combined live class each week for Sigma and Omega. You choose exactly one of three weekly time slots.

Why is the price shown in my currency?

We show an estimate in your local currency, rounded up to the next whole number, so you can compare easily. Plans are priced and billed in Canadian dollars, so your bank's exchange rate applies when you pay.

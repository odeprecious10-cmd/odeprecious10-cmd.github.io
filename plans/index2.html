Before you enroll

How Standard and Flex work

When does a Standard section open?

We start with the primary published time and open further Standard sections as parent demand supports them. A full section closes to new registrations; the next time is selected based on the strongest demand.

What does Flex mean?

Flex offers more approved recurring time windows. It is not unlimited custom scheduling. Families choose from displayed times, helping us match schedules while keeping classes small and consistent.

Can my child take Math and English together?

Yes. Math and English registration open in parallel for cohorts being launched. The subjects have independent section status, so one can be ready while the other is still forming.

Can Flex families reschedule?

Flex includes one schedule transfer per month into another active section of the same subject and cohort, subject to an available seat. It does not create a new one-off lesson. If no seat is available, the normal makeup/alternative process applies.

What if a new class is still forming?

NovaPoint will provide a clear activation decision window and an alternative, such as another suitable active time or the applicable refund, rather than leaving families waiting indefinitely.

Are all subjects twice a week?

Most Math, English, Chemistry and Physics streams are planned for two 60-minute sessions each week. Robotics & Coding is one integrated 60-minute class per week. Choose one available session: Friday evening, Saturday morning or Sunday morning.

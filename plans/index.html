Tuition & Plans · Starts Monday 11 January 2027

Pick the plan. See your class times in your own clock.

Every subject meets twice a week for one live hour, with a strict cap of 5 students. Choose Math, English or both, then choose between Standard, with one published time, and Flex, with a choice of approved weekly windows.

  • 5 student cap
  • 2 live classes a week per subject
  • Prices in CAD per month
  • Secure payment by Stripe

One subject

Math or English, one stream. Choose Standard for the published time, or Flex to pick the weekly window that suits your family.

Two subjects: Math and English

Taking both? Pick the combination that fits your week. If one subject's Standard time does not suit you, take it as Flex and keep the other as Standard. Two classes can never overlap, so times that clash with your first choice are greyed out.

Standard classes run at one published weekly time per grade, with up to 5 students. More Standard times open as classes fill. Flex classes run in approved weekly windows, also with up to 5 students. Every class is 60 minutes, twice a week.

Flex benefit: Flex families can move one class a month into another active class of the same subject and grade, subject to a free seat.

Classes keep their London or Toronto clock time all year. Times above are shown for the first week of Term 1 (11 January 2027). When clocks change in March (Ontario on 14 March, UK on 28 March) your local time can move by one hour for a few weeks.

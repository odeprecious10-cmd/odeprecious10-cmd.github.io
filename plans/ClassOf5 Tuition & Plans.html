Tuition & Plans · Starts Monday 11 January 2027

Pick the plan. See your class times in your own clock.

Every subject meets twice a week for one live hour, with a strict cap of 5 students. Choose Math, English or both, then choose between fixed Standard times and Flex times we open around your family.

  • 5 student cap
  • 2 live classes a week per subject
  • Prices in CAD per month
  • Secure payment by Stripe

One subject

Math or English, one stream. Choose Standard for a published time with 4 to 5 classmates, or Flex for a time that suits you better.

Two subjects: Math and English

Taking both? Pick the combination that fits your week. If one subject's Standard time does not suit you, take it as Flex and keep the other as Standard.

Standard classes run on a published timetable with up to 5 students. Flex classes run at the unpublished or requested times shown here, with up to 3 students, and open once 2 families have enrolled. We tell you the day the class is confirmed.

Classes keep their London or Toronto clock time all year. Times above are shown for the first week of Term 1 (11 January 2027). When clocks change in March (Ontario on 14 March, UK on 28 March) your local time can move by one hour for a few weeks.
